Catch up before starting work:

1. Do the catch-up described in the "Documentation System" section of `AGENTS.md`: read `README.md` and `docs/00_ONBOARDING.md`, then follow onboarding's "Catching Up" steps.
2. Re-read all of `AGENTS.md`, including "Project-Specific Rules", and follow those rules for the rest of the session.
3. Report briefly, based on the **Mode** in `docs/03_TODO.md`. "Your" sessions are the ones whose **Owner** matches `git config user.name`.
   - **Build:** the current phase, the active sessions (yours first) and where they stand, and the next steps.
   - **Maintenance:** the "Now" table, your active sessions and where they stand, and the next tickets. Flag any active session that names no Jira ticket.
